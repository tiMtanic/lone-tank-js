## Math

|Prompt|Summary|
|----------|----------|
|Explain to me in simple steps how I can convert degrees to a direction vector, where 0° result in a direction vector pointing upwards. (0° -> [0, -1])|The AI suggested to swap sine and cosine and negate the y-component:<br />1. Convert degrees to radians<br />2. Compute x and y<br />x = sin(radians)<br />y = -cos(radians)<br />3. Combine them [sin(radians), -cos(radians)]|
|I know I can convert a vector to a rotation in degrees with Math.atan2(x, -y) * 180 / Math.PI. Explain to me the math behind it in simple steps. Keep it short and simple.|The AI explained the math: A vector (x, y) points in some direction.<br />atan2(a, b) returns the angle whose horizontal and vertical components match (b, a). Unlike plain atan, it correctly identifies all four quadrants.<br />Normally, atan2(y, x) measures from the positive x-axis. Using atan2(x, -y) swaps the axes and flips y, so:<br />(0, -1) → 0° (up)<br />(1, 0) → 90° (right)<br />(0, 1) → 180° (down)<br />(-1, 0) → -90° (left)<br />atan2 returns radians, so multiplying by 180 / Math.PI converts radians to degrees.|
